# THE SPECKLED DUCKLING
## Digi World · DW-EBK-021 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by The Ugly Duckling (belonging & growing).
Wisdom: Growing different still means you belong.

Characters: Speck (speckled duckling), Reed (kind swan friend), Pond Kids (ducklings), Elder Swan

### Cover
Title: THE SPECKLED DUCKLING
Subtitle: An Ugly Duckling tale about belonging
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Speck on a lily-pad pond at sunrise; soft mist; other birds in distance; warm golden light; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Pond morning; fluffy ducklings splash; Speck’s feathers are mismatched spots.
SPECK: “Why do I look like a puzzle with extra pieces?”
REED: “Puzzles are cooler with surprise pieces.”

### Page 2
Pond Kids giggle at Speck’s splash style.
KID 1: “You swim zig-zag!”
SPECK: “My zig is still learning its zag.”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
Speck tries a “perfect” dive; lands in a lily crown like a silly hat.
SPECK: “Fashion disaster… or pond royalty?”
REED: “Royalty. Definitely royalty.”

### Page 4
Speck hides in reeds; lonely ripple circles.
SPECK: “Maybe I don’t belong here.”
WIND (soft): “Belonging grows… like you.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Elder Swan glides near; Reed stays close.
ELDER: “Different feathers are still pond family.”
SPECK: “Even speckles?”
ELDER: “Especially speckles.”

### Page 6
Days pass; Speck’s wings strengthen; feathers shine soft silver-cream.
REED: “You’re becoming… wow.”
SPECK: “I’m becoming me. That’s the wow.”

### Page 7
Pond Kids stare kindly this time; invite Speck to race.
KID 2: “Want to lead the splash parade?”
SPECK: “Only if zig-zag is allowed.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
ELDER: “Growing different still means you belong.”
SPECK: “I didn’t need to copy — I needed to keep going.”
REED: “And keep your lily crown for parties.”

### Page 9 — End
Sunset glide across the pond; friends side by side.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Growing different still means you belong.
