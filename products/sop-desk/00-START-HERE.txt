# Digi World SOP & Process Desk — START HERE

Document how your studio runs — hiring, kickoff, delivery, closeout — in one place.
Playbooks with steps and review dates — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **SOP-Process-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name and default categories
2. **Playbooks** — browse sample SOPs (Hiring, Kickoff, Delivery, Closeout)
3. **Editor** — edit steps or add a new playbook
4. **Dashboard** — total SOPs, categories, reviewed this month
5. Set owner and last reviewed date on each playbook
6. Revisit monthly and update steps as your process evolves
