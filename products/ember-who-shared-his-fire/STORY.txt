# EMBER WHO SHARED HIS FIRE
## Digi World · DW-EBK-010 · Ages 3–8
Wisdom: Power feels bigger when you warm someone else — hoarding makes you cold.

Characters: Ember (small red dragon), Mira (shepherd girl), Puff (comic smoke puff), Village kids

### Cover
Title: EMBER WHO SHARED HIS FIRE
Subtitle: A dragon tale about warmth & kindness
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Snowy hill; tiny dragon hugs a single flame in his claws.
EMBER: “If I share, I’ll have none.”
PUFF (smoke): “That’s math for scaredy-dragons.”

### Page 2
Mira finds him shivering by a frozen brook.
MIRA: “Hi. You look like a chili pepper with wings.”
EMBER: “I am fierce… and slightly frozen.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Ember tries a roar; only a polite hiccup of sparks.
PUFF: “Ten out of ten for manners. Zero for scary.”

### Page 4
Village lamps are out; wind stole the fires.
Kids wrap in blankets. Mira asks for help.
MIRA: “Just one spark? We’ll guard it together.”

### Page 5
Ember hides his flame behind a rock.
EMBER: “What if tomorrow is colder?”
Text: Fear makes small flames feel like the last cookie.

### Page 6
Puff sneaks the flame toward Mira — comedy chase, Ember slides on ice.
EMBER: “Traitor smoke!”
PUFF: “Helper smoke. Upgrade.”

### Page 7
Mira cups the spark gently; it doesn’t die — it dances.
MIRA: “Warmth likes company.”
EMBER: “…It got brighter?”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
They light the village hearth together. Ember’s chest glows warmer than before.
Text: Shared fire multiplies. Kept fire only worries.

### Page 9
Funny: kids toast marshmallows; Ember accidentally toasts his own tail tip.
EMBER: “Ow—delicious—ow.”
KID: “You’re the best heater!”

### Page 10
A storm returns; Ember shares sparks with every lamp, laughing.
PUFF: “Look at you, community barbecue.”

### Page 11
Dialogue wise:
EMBER: “I thought giving meant losing.”
MIRA: “Giving meant belonging.”

### Page 12
Ember teaches little dragons (toy-sized cousins) to share sparks with bird nests.

### Page 13
Night feast under lanterns; Ember sleeps on a warm rug, fuller than alone.
Text: The coldest nights need the bravest sharers.

### Page 14 — Closing
Text: Keep your courage like a flame — pass it on, and it won’t go out.
Hoarding makes you small. Warming others makes you mighty.

### Page 15 — End
THE END
Wisdom: Share your gifts. Warmth grows when it travels.
