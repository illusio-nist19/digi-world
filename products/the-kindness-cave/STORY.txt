# THE KINDNESS CAVE
## Digi World · DW-EBK-009 · Ages 3–8
Wisdom: Real treasure grows when you share it — greed shrinks everything.

Characters: Zara (clever girl), Nib (funny goat), Echo (cave whisper), Shadow-Thieves (comic bunglers)

### Cover
Title: THE KINDNESS CAVE
Subtitle: A treasure adventure about sharing
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Desert path at golden hour; Zara and Nib trek toward rocky hills.
“Treasure maps make my tummy flutter!” Zara grins.
NIB: “My tummy flutters for snacks. Same thing.”

### Page 2
They find a carved door in the rock: Open for those who give.
ZARA: “Give… what? We only brought crumbs.”
NIB: “I can give a dramatic sneeze.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Nib sneezes; door giggles open a crack.
ECHO (voice): “Not sneeze-magic. Heart-magic.”
NIB: “Rude. Accurate. Rude.”

### Page 4
Inside: glowing jewels, but sticky shadows grab greedily and drop everything.
Text: The Shadow-Thieves grab and grab — and somehow keep less.
SHADOW 1: “Mine!”
SHADOW 2: “Mine-r!”
Both trip. Jewels bounce back to the pile.

### Page 5
Zara offers her last apricot to a tiny cave mouse.
ECHO: “One kind bite opens ten bright paths.”
The cave lights a soft gold road.

### Page 6
Funny: Nib tries to “share” by licking a jewel.
NIB: “I shared spit!”
ZARA: “That is… not the lesson.”

### Page 7
They meet Echo, a friendly cave spirit of mist and stars.
ECHO: “Greed makes pockets heavy and hearts tiny.”
ZARA: “Then we take only what we need… and leave more for others?”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
They fill a small pouch for home, leave a feast of fruit for travelers, polish a jewel for the mouse.
Text: Sharing didn’t empty the cave. It made the glow grow louder.

### Page 9
Shadow-Thieves watch, confused, then copy-share a banana (badly).
SHADOW 1: “I share… by throwing?”
NIB: “Aim is optional. Kindness isn’t.”

### Page 10
The cave gifts them a lantern that lights when they help someone.
ECHO: “This light works only for helpers. Perfect for you two.”

### Page 11 — Funny dialogue
NIB: “Does it light for snack-helpers?”
ECHO: “If the snack is shared.”
NIB: “Suddenly I’m generous.”

### Page 12
Homeward under stars; Zara gives half her water to a thirsty traveler.
The lantern blooms bright.

### Page 13
Village feast; kids pass bread; Nib “accidentally” shares his best leaf.
ZARA: “See? Treasure that moves stays warm.”

### Page 14 — Closing wisdom
Text: Keepers of gold grow tired. Keepers of kindness grow brave.
The best caves aren’t secret — they’re open.

### Page 15 — End
THE END
Wisdom: Share what you can. Leave the world brighter than you found it.
