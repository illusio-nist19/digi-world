## The job this kit does

When a customer asks *“How do you govern AI?”* you send a **dated one-pager** and, if they insist, a **restricted evidence tree** — not a panic Slack thread.

## What this kit does not do

- Make you EU AI Act / ISO 42001 / NIST “compliant”
- Replace counsel
- Safe-harbour a high-risk hiring or credit engine
