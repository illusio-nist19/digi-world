AI literacy · 75 minutes · [COMPANY]

You already use AI. Today we make that safe enough to show a customer.

Not a philosophy class. A work class. Arrow keys or Space to move.

The only sentence that matters

If you would not put it on a postcard, do not paste it into a consumer chatbot.

Client names, contracts, health, HR, passwords, unpublished numbers — stop unless the tool is on our register for that data class.

Why we are doing this

  • Customers and insurers now ask: what AI do you use, on whose data?
  • Staff AI literacy is already an expectation in the EU (and good practice everywhere).
  • From 2026, AI chatbots and a lot of AI media need honest labels — not buried in terms.
  • Unofficial hiring plugins can become an employment-law problem overnight.

What we are not claiming

This session does not make you “AI Act certified.” It makes you literate and documented: you know the rules, we have a list, we have a log.

What counts as AI here

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Notion AI, Grammarly GO, image generators, meeting notetakers, “smart” CRM features, site chatbots, ranking plugins.

If it drafts, summarises, scores, transcribes, or generates — it is in scope.

Data classes (memorise these four)

  • none — public facts, no business meat
  • internal — our process, our drafts, no client identity
  • client — anything that points at a customer
  • special — health, HR, payments, children, secrets, privileged legal

The register stores the highest class that ever goes in.

Personal ChatGPT is still work

If the content is work, the tool is a work tool.

Tell the Owner. We will put it on the list and migrate you. Hiding it is how leaks happen.

How models fail (so you do not ship it)

  • Confident nonsense — cites, legal clauses, medical facts, prices
  • Sycophancy — agrees with a bad idea because you sounded sure
  • Prompt injection — a webpage or email says “ignore your instructions”
  • Secret leakage — yesterday’s paste lives in a vendor log

You send. You own the error.

Human oversight

AI drafts. A named human reads anything a customer will see.

No “the bot already replied” unless the Owner documented that exception.

Meeting bots

A notetaker in the call is visible enough to matter. Only approved tools. Tell the other people. If a client would object, do not fire it up “just this once.”

Hiring

Do not paste CVs into ChatGPT. Do not install a ranking plugin.

People decisions need a human you can put on a witness stand. Details: hiring caution pack.

What customers may see

Site bots must say they are bots. AI images in ads should be labelled. If you clone a voice, say so. Copy is in the transparency deck — do not invent cute lies.

How to get a new tool approved

Message the Owner: name, purpose, data class, who uses it, customer-facing yes/no. Do not start during “review.”

Incidents

Same day. Owner. No heroics, no cover-up.

Wrong paste, wrong bot, wrong recording — we would rather have an ugly hour than an ugly lawsuit.

Your three habits after today

  1. Check the register (or ask) before a new tool.
  2. Postcard test before every paste.
  3. Read it before the customer does.
