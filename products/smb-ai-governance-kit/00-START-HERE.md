# SMB AI Governance Kit — Start here

**Time to first usable pack: 90 minutes.**  
**Time to a pack you can email a client: one working day.**

This kit exists because most small companies already use AI and have **nothing they can show** when a buyer, insurer, or board asks: *What AI do you use, on whose data, with whose approval?*

You are not buying a certificate. You are installing a **register, a policy, a training log, and an evidence folder** — the four things that stop that question from becoming a fire drill.

## What “done” looks like

After setup you can answer yes to all of these:

1. We have a **named owner** for AI in this company.
2. We have a **list of every AI tool** we actually use (including the unofficial ones).
3. Every tool has a **data class** (none / internal / client / special).
4. Staff have a **one-page rule** for what they may paste into a model.
5. Staff who use AI have a **dated training record**.
6. If we publish AI-made content, we have **disclosure copy** ready.
7. If something goes wrong, we know **who to call in the first hour**.
8. There is a **quarterly 30-minute review** on the calendar.

If you cannot show those eight artefacts, you do not have governance. You have a slide.

## Who this is for

Companies of roughly **5–50 people** that:

- use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Midjourney, or similar
- sell to the EU/UK, or to a larger company that sends vendor questionnaires
- are mostly **users (deployers)** of someone else’s model, not builders of hiring/credit/biometric engines

If you **build** a high-risk AI product (CV screening as a product, credit scoring, biometrics), this kit is **not enough**. You need counsel and a proper technical file. The kit still helps you inventory what you use internally.

## Who this is not for

- A hospital, bank, or government deploying high-risk AI as a core system
- Anyone who wants a stamp that says “EU AI Act certified”
- Resellers (the licence is **one organisation, internal use**)

## The only rule that matters on day one

**Do not paste client names, medical facts, HR files, passwords, or unpublished financials into a consumer chatbot unless the owner has approved that tool for that data class.**

Everything else in this kit exists to make that rule real, recorded, and repeatable.
