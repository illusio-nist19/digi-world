# Human oversight — one page

**Company:** [COMPANY]

AI output is a **draft**. A named human is accountable for:

| If the output will… | Then before it ships… |
| --- | --- |
| Reach a customer | Named reviewer reads it (`human_oversight_name` on that register row) |
| Change money, contracts, or access | Second person or Owner reviews |
| Affect hiring, promotion, or firing | Counsel + Owner — default is **no AI decision** |
| Be published as media | Transparency copy applied; reviewer checks likeness/rights |

## Override

Anyone may **stop** an AI-assisted send. They message the Owner with the tool_id and a one-line reason. Status may move to `paused` the same day.

## Logs

Where the vendor provides logs (ChatGPT Team/Enterprise, Copilot admin), the Owner reviews **spot samples** at the quarterly meeting — not every prompt.

Where there are no logs (personal free ChatGPT), that is a **reason to pause** client-class use.
