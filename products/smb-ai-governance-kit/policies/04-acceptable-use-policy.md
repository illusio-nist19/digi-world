# Acceptable use of AI — [COMPANY]

**Effective:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]  
**Owner:** [AI OWNER NAME]  
**Applies to:** all workers, contractors, and interns using company systems or doing company work.

This is a **work rule**, not a philosophy essay. If it is not on the approved register at your data class, do not use it for that data.

## 1. Default rule

You may use **approved** AI tools for the **purpose and data class** listed on the AI system register.

You may **not**:

- put **client**, **special**, or confidential data into a tool that is not approved for that class
- turn on a new AI plugin, notetaker, or “smart” feature and hope for the best
- pretend a personal ChatGPT account is “not work” if the content is work
- publish AI output as if a human produced it when disclosure is required (see transparency pack)
- use AI to **score, rank, or reject people** for jobs unless the Owner and counsel have approved that exact tool

## 2. Never paste (unless Owner has approved a specific enterprise tool in writing)

- Passwords, keys, badges, MFA codes
- Full identification numbers, payment card data, bank accounts
- Health, diagnosis, or insurance details
- HR files: performance, discipline, salaries, union activity
- Children’s data
- Privileged legal advice
- Customer data you would not put on a postcard

**When in doubt, strip names** and ask the Owner *before* you paste.

## 3. Human stays responsible

AI drafts. **You** send. If a customer email, contract clause, clinical note, or financial number is wrong, “the model said so” is not a defence.

For customer-facing output: a named human must **read it** before it goes out, unless the Owner has documented an exception (e.g. a FAQ bot with a hard escalation path).

## 4. Meeting notetakers

A bot in a call is **customer-facing enough to matter**.

- Only use a notetaker on the register with status approved/conditional.
- Tell other participants (and clients) that a notetaker is present, unless a documented exception exists.
- Do not record a call in a country/context where you lack a lawful basis. Ask operations if unsure.

## 5. Personal accounts

Work belongs in **work accounts** once they exist. If you used a personal AI account for work before this policy, tell the Owner in the survey. We will migrate, not stage a trial.

## 6. Banned uses (stop and escalate)

Do not use AI to:

- identify people from photos/video in public without a clear lawful basis and Owner approval
- build a “trust” or “risk” score on people as social scoring
- generate non-consensual intimate imagery
- cheat a customer, regulator, exam, or certification
- automate a hiring reject without a documented, counsel-reviewed process

## 7. How to request a new tool

Message the Owner with: tool name, purpose, data class, vendor, who will use it, whether customers see it. **Do not start using it** while status is `review`.

## 8. Incidents

If client data went into the wrong tool, or AI caused a customer-facing error: follow `incidents/08-incident-playbook.md` **the same day**. You will not be punished for fast reporting. You may be for hiding it.

## 9. Training

If you use AI at work, you complete the company AI literacy session and appear on the attendance log. No log, no use.

## 10. Changes

The Owner may tighten this policy immediately for a specific tool (pause). Broader rewrites need the policy approver.
