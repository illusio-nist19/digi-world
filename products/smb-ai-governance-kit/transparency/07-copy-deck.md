# Transparency copy deck

Use this when `customer_facing = yes`. Edit tone; keep the **fact** that a person may be interacting with AI or looking at AI-made media.

**Not legal advice.** EU Article 50-style transparency (labelling AI interactions and certain AI media) is a moving target — confirm current rules with counsel before you publish in the EU.

## Website chatbot

> You are chatting with an automated assistant. It can make mistakes. For account or legal questions, ask for a human. Do not send passwords or sensitive documents here.

## Email / proposal footer (AI-assisted, human-sent)

> This document was drafted with assistance from AI tools and reviewed by [NAME / TEAM] at [COMPANY] before sending.

## Published images / ads

> Image generated or heavily edited using AI.

If a real person’s likeness appears: you need **rights**, not just a caption.

## Podcast / video clone or avatar

> Voice/appearance is AI-generated. It does not depict a live recording of [NAME] unless stated.

## Support macros

Train staff: if the bot fails, the human says:

> I’m [NAME], a person. I’ll take it from here.

## Checklist before go-live

- [ ] Bot states it is not human at the start, not buried in T&Cs only
- [ ] Escalation to a human exists and is tested
- [ ] No medical, legal, or credit advice from the bot
- [ ] Marketing page matches what the bot actually does
- [ ] Register row `customer_facing = yes` and oversight named

File screenshots of live disclosures in `evidence/08-transparency/`.
