## What the Owner is accountable for

- Keeping the **AI system register** true
- Approving (or refusing) tools that touch **client** or **special** data
- Running **literacy** training and keeping the attendance log
- Chairing the **quarterly review**
- Being the person a staff member can ping: *“Can I paste this into ChatGPT?”*

The Owner may delegate tasks. They may not delegate **accountability**.

## What the Owner is not

- Your lawyer
- Required to review every prompt
- Automatically “Head of IT”

## Escalation

If the Owner is unavailable for more than **two working days**, the Deputy acts with full authority.

If Owner and Deputy disagree on a **pause**, the **stricter** decision wins until the policy approver decides.
