**Honest answers help. We are not hunting people.** If a tool is risky we will find a safer way or an approved alternative — not a public shaming.

Please reply by **[DEADLINE]** or fill the form: **[FORM LINK]**

1. Which AI tools have you used for work in the last 90 days? (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, Midjourney, Notion AI, Grammarly, Otter, Fireflies, Canva Magic, browser plugins, other — name them.)

5. Have you used a **meeting notetaker** that joins calls? Which one? Are clients told?
6. Do you use AI in anything **customers see**? (site chat, ads, blogs, proposals, images, voices)
7. Is there a tool you **wish** were approved so you could stop using a personal account?
8. Anything else we should know?

## How the Owner processes answers

- One row in the register per distinct tool (not per person).
- If three people use ChatGPT, **one row**, data class = highest reported.
- Personal ChatGPT Plus used for work **still goes on the register**.
- “Not sure” on client data → treat as `client` until proven otherwise.

Store raw (anonymised if you prefer) responses in `evidence/03-discovery/` for 12 months.
