This page describes how we **govern staff use of third-party AI**. It is not a legal opinion, not an audit report, and not a certification under the EU AI Act or any other law.

## How we work

- We keep an **AI system register** (tools, purpose, data class, named human oversight, approval status).
- Staff follow a short **acceptable-use policy**. Client and sensitive data do not go into consumer chatbots unless a specific tool is approved for that class.
- People who use AI complete **literacy training**; we keep an attendance log.
- Customer-facing bots and AI media are **labelled**; a human remains accountable for what we send.
- We review the register at least **quarterly** and when we add a tool.
- Hiring, credit, biometric, or other high-risk AI is **not** in casual use; those would require separate legal review.
