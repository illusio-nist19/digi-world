# Digi World Expense & Tax Desk — START HERE

Track business spend and stay tax-ready without another monthly app.
Categories, receipts notes, and CSV export — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Expense-Tax-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name, currency, and expense categories
2. **Dashboard** — this month spend, by category, tax-ready total
3. **Add expense** — date, vendor, category, amount, notes
4. Log every business purchase as it happens
5. **Export CSV** — hand to your accountant at year-end
6. Review categories in Setup when your business changes
