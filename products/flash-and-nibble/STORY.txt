# FLASH AND NIBBLE
## Digi World · DW-EBK-015 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by the Tortoise and the Hare race.
Wisdom: Slow and steady finishes strong — rushing trips on its own feet.

Characters: Flash (speedy rabbit), Nibble (calm tortoise), Crow Coach, Finish Flag

### Cover
Title: FLASH AND NIBBLE
Subtitle: A race story about patience
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Rabbit in sneakers racing ahead; smiling tortoise with a tiny scarf; sunny meadow finish line in distance; bold colorful title.

### Page 1
Meadow morning; race banner flaps.
CROW: “Ready… set… snack? I mean GO!”
FLASH: “I’m already finished in my brain!”
NIBBLE: “I’ll start with one step.”

### Page 2
Flash zooms past flowers; Nibble walks steadily.
FLASH: “See you yesterday, Shell!”
NIBBLE: “See you… eventually.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Flash naps under a lemon tree “for a second.”
FLASH: “Winning needs beauty sleep.”
A bee lands on his nose. FLASH: “AH—cute alarm!”

### Page 4
Nibble passes the sleeping Flash, humming.
NIBBLE: “Step. Breathe. Step. Breathe.”
CROW (above): “Looking strong, slowpoke hero!”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Flash wakes, panics, runs crooked.
FLASH: “Where’s the finish? Where’s my dignity?!”
TREE: “Left at the daisy. Dignity sold separately.”

### Page 6
Nibble climbs a tiny hill carefully.
NIBBLE: “Hills are just tall floors.”
FLASH zoom-past then trip-sprawl in mud.
FLASH: “I invented a new sport: face-planting.”

### Page 7
Near the flag, Flash tries one last sprint.
Nibble places one careful foot over the line first.
CROW: “Winner: Nibble!”
FLASH: “My legs filed a complaint.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
NIBBLE: “Fast is fun. Finish is kinder.”
FLASH: “Teach me the breathe-step thing?”
NIBBLE: “Lesson one: don’t nap mid-win.”

### Page 9 — End
They share carrots at sunset; friends, not foes.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Slow and steady finishes strong.
