# CINDERS AND THE KIND SLIPPERS
## Digi World · DW-EBK-022 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by Cinderella (kindness is the real magic).
Wisdom: Kindness is the real glass slipper.

Characters: Cinders (kind girl), Pippa the sparrow, Fairy Aunt Glow, Prince Theo (friendly kid prince), Stepsisters (comic, not mean-scary)

### Cover
Title: CINDERS AND THE KIND SLIPPERS
Subtitle: A Cinderella story about real magic
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Cinders in a soft blue dress holding glowing crystal slippers; Pippa on her shoulder; pumpkin coach glow in background; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Kitchen morning; Cinders shares crumbs with Pippa while sweeping sparkles.
CINDERS: “Kindness fits every shoe size.”
PIPPA: “Including bird feet. Confirmed.”

### Page 2
Invitation to the village kindness ball arrives; stepsisters grab fancy ribbons.
SIS 1: “We’ll shine the loudest!”
CINDERS: “I’ll shine by helping… if I can go.”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
Stepsisters pile hats; hats collapse into a silly tower.
SIS 2: “Fashion emergency!”
PIPPA: “That tower needs a building permit.”

### Page 4
Fairy Aunt Glow appears with warm lantern light — not scary magic.
GLOW: “Wish granted for a heart that already shares.”
CINDERS: “May I please go… and still come home kind?”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Crystal slippers appear; soft blue dress; pumpkin coach giggles.
SLIPPER: “We only fit feet that walk gently.”
CINDERS: “Then I’m ready.”

### Page 6
At the ball, Cinders helps a dropped tray; Theo laughs and helps too.
THEO: “You fix messes with smiles.”
CINDERS: “Messes are just parties that need teamwork.”

### Page 7
Midnight soft chime; one slipper stays on the stair as she rushes kindly home.
PIPPA: “Left a clue made of kindness!”
GLOW: “The right heart will find it.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
Theo returns the slipper; it fits Cinders perfectly.
THEO: “Kindness is the real glass slipper.”
CINDERS: “And it never pinches.”

### Page 9 — End
Village feast; everyone shares cake — even the hat-tower sisters.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Kindness is the real glass slipper.
