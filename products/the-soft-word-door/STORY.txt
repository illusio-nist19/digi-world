# THE SOFT-WORD DOOR
## Digi World · DW-EBK-014 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by the Open-Sesame treasure-door adventure (Ali Baba–style).
Wisdom: Kind words open doors that greed cannot.

Characters: Mira (clever girl), Pip the kitten, the Stone Door, Greedy Shadows (comic)

### Cover
Title: THE SOFT-WORD DOOR
Subtitle: An Open-Sesame adventure about kind words
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Mira and Pip before a glowing carved stone door in a mossy canyon; golden light in the crack; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Mira and Pip hike a sunny canyon path with a crumpled map.
MIRA: “The map says treasure… if the door will listen.”
PIP: “Doors have ears? Rude doors.”

### Page 2
They find a huge stone door carved with vines and stars.
DOOR (whisper): “Speak the soft word… not the shouty one.”
MIRA: “Soft word?”
PIP: “I know shouty. Meow-MEOW!”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
Pip yells “OPEN!” The door stays shut; pebbles giggle.
SHADOW: “Say MINE! Louder!”
MIRA: “That sounds like a trap that wears a cape.”

### Page 4
They try greed words: “Give!” “Now!” “All for us!”
Nothing. Pip’s whiskers droop.
PIP: “My shout ran out of batteries.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Mira kneels, paw on stone.
MIRA: “Please… may we enter gently?”
DOOR: soft glow blooms along the carvings.
PIP: “Please worked? I’m adding please to breakfast.”

### Page 6
Inside: glowing coins and gems; Shadows grab and drop everything.
SHADOW 1: “Mine!”
SHADOW 2: “Mine-er!”
Both trip. Treasure rolls back to the pile.

### Page 7
Mira shares her last apricot with a tiny cave mouse.
MIRA: “Thank you for letting us visit.”
The cave lights a golden path forward.

### Page 8 — Wisdom
DOOR: “Greed bangs. Kindness knocks.”
PIP: “And please is the secret key?”
DOOR: “Yes. Soft words open soft worlds.”

### Page 9 — End
They leave with one small lantern and sticky apricot smiles.
MIRA: “We didn’t empty the cave. We woke its glow.”
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Kind words open doors that greed cannot.
