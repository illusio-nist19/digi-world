# Digi World · Little Cozy Days — START HERE

A printable coloring ebook for ages 3–4.
18 bold-line scenes + a full-color cover. Minimalist, cute, and cozy.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Open **Little-Cozy-Days.pdf**
3. Print pages at home on letter or A4 paper (one side)

## Inside
- Color cover (print or keep as screen preview)
- 18 black-and-white scenes:
  home · bedroom · kitchen · classroom · reading · garden
  playground · pilot · doctor · nurse · firefighter · chef
  teacher · beach · rainy day · puppy · birthday · family night

## Tips for little hands
- Thick markers or crayons work best
- Print one page at a time if your printer is small
- Supervise scissors if you cut pages out
