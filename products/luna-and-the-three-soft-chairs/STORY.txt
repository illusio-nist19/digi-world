# LUNA AND THE THREE SOFT CHAIRS
## Digi World · DW-EBK-019 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by Goldilocks and the Three Bears (respect & asking first).
Wisdom: Ask before you borrow — kindness keeps homes warm.

Characters: Luna (curious girl), Dot the duckling, Mama Bear, Papa Bear, Baby Bear

### Cover
Title: LUNA AND THE THREE SOFT CHAIRS
Subtitle: A Goldilocks adventure about asking first
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Luna and Dot at a cozy cottage doorway with three glowing chairs of different sizes; warm firelight; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Sunny forest path; Luna skips with a berry basket; Dot waddles behind.
LUNA: “Someone’s cottage smells like cinnamon soup!”
DOT: “My tummy filed a report. Investigation: snacks.”

### Page 2
Inside: three bowls steam on a wooden table.
LUNA: “Too hot… too cold… oh—this one is just right!”
DOT: “She didn’t ask the bowls. Rude bowls? Rude us?”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
Luna plops into Papa’s huge chair; it squeaks and tips.
CHAIR: “Oof! I am furniture, not a trampoline!”
DOT: “Discovery: chairs have feelings and backaches.”

### Page 4
She tries Mama’s medium chair — too bouncy; feathers fly.
LUNA: “Whee—wait—whoops!”
DOT: “We are making a feather festival without invitations.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Baby Bear’s small soft chair fits; Luna almost naps… then freezes.
LUNA: “Dot… did we ask permission?”
DOT: “Permission is the secret spice. We forgot the spice.”

### Page 6
The Bear family returns with berry jars; surprised, then gentle.
BABY BEAR: “You sat in my chair… but you look sorry.”
MAMA BEAR: “Sorry is a start. Asking is the full recipe.”

### Page 7
Luna offers her berry basket; Dot bows with a comic flourish.
LUNA: “May we please share these… and help tidy the feathers?”
PAPA BEAR: “Please unlocked the door better than knocking.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
MAMA BEAR: “Homes stay warm when guests ask first.”
LUNA: “Next time I’ll knock with my words before my feet.”
DOT: “And my tummy will wait for an RSVP.”

### Page 9 — End
They picnic outside; three chairs glow softly by the window.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Ask before you borrow — kindness keeps homes warm.
