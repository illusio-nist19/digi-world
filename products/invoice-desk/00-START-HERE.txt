# Digi World Quote & Invoice Desk — START HERE

High-demand money desk for freelancers and small studios: quote → invoice → get paid.
No Google Sheets. No subscription. Data stays on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Invoice-Quote-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name, email, currency, tax %, payment terms, invoice prefix
2. **Dashboard** — open quotes, unpaid invoices, overdue total
3. **New quote** — client + line items → Save quote
4. **Convert** — turn an accepted quote into an invoice in one click
5. **Print / PDF** — open the document → Print → Save as PDF
6. Mark invoices **Paid** when the money lands
