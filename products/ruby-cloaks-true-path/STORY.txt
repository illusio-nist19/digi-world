# RUBY CLOAK'S TRUE PATH
## Digi World · DW-EBK-017 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by the forest-path journey (listen, don’t take the risky shortcut).
Wisdom: The longer kind path is safer than the shiny shortcut.

Characters: Ruby (girl in red cloak), Dot the duck, Grandma Grove, Trick Path (tempting)

### Cover
Title: RUBY CLOAK'S TRUE PATH

### Page 1
Cottage morning; pie for Grandma Grove.
MUM: “Stay on the marked path. No glitter shortcuts.”
RUBY: “Marked path. Got it.”
DOT: “I will supervise. Quack authority.”

### Page 2
Forest trail with painted stones.
RUBY: “Left stone, right stone… boring stones.”
DOT: “Boring keeps pies intact.”

### Page 3 — Temptation
A sparkly side trail whispers.
TRICK PATH: “I’m faster! I’m famous! I’m… unsupervised!”
RUBY: “Mum said marked path…”
DOT: “Also pie insurance prefers boring.”

### Page 4 — Funny almost-fail
Ruby takes two sparkly steps; vines tickle; basket wobbles.
RUBY: “AH—the shortcut has opinions!”
DOT: “Quack retreat! Quack retreat!”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
They return to painted stones.
RUBY: “Listening is a superpower that doesn’t need a cape.”
DOT: “Your cloak is already a cape. Confusing.”

### Page 6
They meet a lost hedgehog; share water; keep walking.
HEDGEHOG: “Thanks. Shortcuts stole my sense of direction.”
RUBY: “Join the marked-path club.”

### Page 7
Arrive at Grandma Grove’s warm door; pie safe.
GRANDMA: “You took the true path.”
RUBY: “The shiny one tried to hire me. I declined.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
GRANDMA: “Fast roads can hide thorns. Kind roads show lanterns.”
DOT: “And protect pie crust integrity.”

### Page 9 — End
Tea, crumbs, sunset through trees.
Text: THE END · Digi World
