# THREE LITTLE NEST BUILDERS
## Digi World · DW-EBK-018 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by the Three Little Pigs building story.
Wisdom: Careful work stands when the wind arrives.

Characters: Pip, Pop, Puff (bird siblings), Wind Wolf (blustery comic), Strong Nest

### Cover
Title: THREE LITTLE NEST BUILDERS
Subtitle: A build-it-right adventure
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Three cute bird siblings; two flimsy nests blowing; one sturdy nest glowing warm; comic wind cloud; bold colorful title.

### Page 1
Spring morning in an oak tree.
PIP: “I’ll build with leaves. Instant nest!”
POP: “I’ll build with twigs… mostly.”
PUFF: “I’ll build slow. And strong.”

### Page 2
Pip’s leafy nest looks fluffy and fashionable.
PIP: “Done before snack!”
POP: “Mine is… modern art.”
PUFF: measures, ties, checks.

### Page 3 — Funny
Wind Wolf huffs gently.
WIND: “Testing… one… two… HUFF!”
Pip’s nest becomes a leaf parade.
PIP: “My nest went traveling without me!”

### Page 4
Pop’s twig nest wobbles, collapses like spaghetti.
POP: “It was open-concept. Too open.”
WIND: “I provide free demolition.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Puff’s nest holds: woven, mud-sealed, cozy.
PUFF: “Strong isn’t fancy. Strong is finished.”
PIP: “Teach us your boring magic!”
PUFF: “Step one: don’t rush step one.”

### Page 6
They rebuild together with good materials.
POP: “Pass the sturdy twig.”
PIP: “I brought leaves for decoration only.”
WIND: “I’m watching. Professionally.”

### Page 7
Big storm night; nest stays put; siblings huddle.
PUFF: “Hear that? That’s the sound of prep.”
PIP: “Also my tummy. But mostly prep.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
WIND (softer): “Even I respect a job done right.”
PUFF: “Careful work stands when the wind arrives.”

### Page 9 — End
Sunny morning; cocoa worms (for birds); high-five wings.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Careful work stands when the wind arrives.
