# Digi World Contract & Agreement Desk — START HERE

Track client contracts, renewals, and signatures without another SaaS bill.
Clients, contract status, and print-ready previews — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Contract-Agreement-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name and currency
2. **Clients** — add the studios and brands you contract with
3. **Dashboard** — active contracts, pending signatures, renewals due
4. **Editor** — create a contract with dates, value, and notes
5. **Print / PDF** — preview and save a clean copy for records
6. Mark status as draft → sent → signed as deals progress
