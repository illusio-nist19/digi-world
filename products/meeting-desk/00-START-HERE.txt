# Digi World Meeting & Follow-up Desk — START HERE

Never lose an action item after a client call again.
Meetings, notes, and follow-ups — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Meeting-Followup-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Dashboard** — meetings this week, open follow-ups
2. **Log a meeting** — date, client, attendees, notes
3. Add **action items** inside each meeting
4. **Follow-ups** tab — due dates and done toggles
5. Check follow-ups every morning until the list is clear
6. Mark items **done** the moment you send or finish them
