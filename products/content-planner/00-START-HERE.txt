# Digi World Content & Social Planner — START HERE

Plan posts, drafts, and ideas without a social scheduler subscription.
Calendar, captions, and an ideas board — all on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Content-Social-Planner.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Dashboard** — scheduled this week, drafts, published count
2. **Calendar** — month view of what goes out when
3. **New post** — platform, title, caption, scheduled date
4. Move posts from **draft** → **scheduled** → **published**
5. **Ideas board** — capture hooks before they disappear
6. Check the calendar every Monday for the week ahead
