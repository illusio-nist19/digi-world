# THE WHISPERING MARKET
## Digi World · DW-EBK-012 · Ages 3–8
Wisdom: Listen before you leap — hurried choices make messy days; listening makes wise ones.

Characters: Samir (boy), Tala (sister), Baza (talking parrot), Hasty Hare (comic), Patient Owl

### Cover
Title: THE WHISPERING MARKET
Subtitle: A bazaar adventure about listening well
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Busy colorful market; spices, carpets, bells.
SAMIR: “First stall wins!”
TALA: “First listen wins.”

### Page 2
Baza the parrot rides Samir’s shoulder.
BAZA: “Squawk! Rushing is my cardio!”
TALA: “And my headache.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Hasty Hare zooms, knocks spice jars; sneeze cloud.
HARE: “No time for labels!”
SAMIR: (sneezing) “Was that cinnamon or chaos?”

### Page 4
A whisper stall sells “answers” — but only if you ask slowly.
OWL shopkeeper: “Questions that run arrive empty.”

### Page 5
Samir blurts: “Where’s the magic key?!”
Stall goes quiet. Nothing appears.
OWL: “I heard noise. Not a question.”

### Page 6
Tala kneels, breathes, asks: “What does the key open that helps everyone?”
A silver ribbon map unrolls.

### Page 7 — Funny
Baza tries “wise voice”:
BAZA: “Ahem. Philosophical cracker, please.”
OWL: “One cracker. Zero wisdom. Try again.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
They follow the map by listening to vendors: water seller, cobbler, child humming.
Text: The market speaks in small truths. Hurry only hears its own feet.

### Page 9
Hasty Hare grabs a shiny false key; it squeaks and squirts water.
HARE: “Betrayal!”
SAMIR: “Maybe… labels matter.”

### Page 10
True key is wooden, plain, found after helping a lost duckling home.
TALA: “Help first. Treasure second.”

### Page 11
Key opens a community cupboard of spare shoes, bread, umbrellas.
OWL: “Listening built this. Not racing.”

### Page 12
Dialogue:
SAMIR: “I wanted to be fastest.”
TALA: “You became useful. Better.”
BAZA: “Squawk. Character development!”

### Page 13
They teach Hare to pause-count to three before grabbing.
HARE: “One… two… still want it… three… ask.”

### Page 14 — Closing
Text: Wise kids hear more than they shout.
Slow ears find doors that fast feet miss.

### Page 15 — End
THE END
Wisdom: Pause. Listen. Then choose — that’s how futures stay kind.
