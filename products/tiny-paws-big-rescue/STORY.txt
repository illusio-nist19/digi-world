# TINY PAW'S BIG RESCUE
## Digi World · DW-EBK-023 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by The Lion and the Mouse (small helpers & promises).
Wisdom: Even tiny paws can keep a big promise.

Characters: Miri (tiny mouse), Leo (young lion), Net Trap (comic villain prop), Forest Friends

### Cover
Title: TINY PAW'S BIG RESCUE
Subtitle: A Lion & Mouse adventure about promises
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Tiny mouse Miri on Leo the lion’s paw in a sunlit savanna clearing; warm golden light; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Savanna morning; Miri squeaks across Leo’s nose while he naps.
MIRI: “Shortcut! Express route: lion highway!”
LEO (sleepy): “Tiny traffic… on my face?”

### Page 2
Leo lifts a paw; Miri freezes with comic big eyes.
LEO: “I could… but I won’t. Go free.”
MIRI: “I owe you one promise the size of me!”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
Miri tries to “train” for rescue day with a leaf cape.
MIRI: “Hero mode: activated!”
LEAF: “I am a leaf. Please stop.”

### Page 4
Hunters’ rope net snags Leo; he roars, stuck and embarrassed.
LEO: “I am too big for this knotty nonsense!”
MIRI: “Perfect job for tiny teeth.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
Miri chews rope by rope; Leo whispers encouragement.
LEO: “Steady… you’re doing giant work.”
MIRI: “Promises taste like rope. Weirdly motivating.”

### Page 6
The net falls; Leo is free; forest friends cheer.
FRIEND: “Tiny paws win!”
LEO: “Size is loud. Help is louder.”

### Page 7
Leo shares shade and fruit; Miri wears a tiny honor leaf (non-cape).
MIRI: “I kept my word.”
LEO: “And I learned to listen to small voices.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
LEO: “Even tiny paws can keep a big promise.”
MIRI: “And big lions can choose kindness first.”
FRIENDS: “Team Tiny-and-Roar forever!”

### Page 9 — End
Sunset walk: mouse on lion’s shoulder, both smiling.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Even tiny paws can keep a big promise.
