# Digi World Subscription Audit Desk — START HERE

See what your studio spends on software every month and what to cut.
Monthly burn, renewals, and cancel savings — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Subscription-Audit-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name and currency
2. **Subscriptions** — review sample tools (Adobe, Notion, Spotify…)
3. **Editor** — add your real subscriptions with amount and billing cycle
4. **Dashboard** — monthly burn, yearly estimate, cancel savings
5. Mark status keep / review / cancel as you audit
6. **Export CSV** — share with your bookkeeper or team
