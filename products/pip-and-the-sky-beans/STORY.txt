# PIP AND THE SKY BEANS
## Digi World · DW-EBK-020 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by Jack and the Beanstalk (courage without greed).
Wisdom: Courage climbs; greed falls.

Characters: Pip (brave boy), Nib the mouse, Cloud Giant (gentle comic), Magic Beans

### Cover
Title: PIP AND THE SKY BEANS
Subtitle: A beanstalk adventure about brave hearts
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Pip and Nib at the base of a glowing green beanstalk twisting into golden clouds; title large and colorful.

### Page 1
Farmyard dawn; Pip holds shiny beans; cow watches skeptically.
PIP: “These beans hum like they know the sky’s address.”
NIB: “If they know Wi-Fi, I’m moving in.”

### Page 2
Overnight the beans sprout into a towering leafy ladder.
PIP: “Courage first step… then the next.”
NIB: “I packed bravery snacks. Also cheese. Mostly cheese.”

### Page 3 — Funny fail
They slip on a dewy leaf; Nib hangs from Pip’s scarf.
NIB: “Gravity filed a complaint!”
PIP: “Hold on—adventure includes slapstick.”

### Page 4
Above the clouds: a castle of soft cotton and golden windows.
GIANT (gentle boom): “WHO smells… curiosity?”
PIP: “Just visitors who knock with courage, not grabby hands.”

### Page 5 — Dialogue
A glowing harp sings; a greedy shadow-hand reaches from fog.
SHADOW: “Take the gold! All of it!”
PIP: “No. We came to ask… not to empty the sky.”

### Page 6
The Giant smiles; the shadow melts; beans sparkle brighter.
GIANT: “Brave hearts climb. Greedy hearts slip.”
NIB: “I almost slipped on cheese. Same science.”

### Page 7
The Giant gifts one singing seed and a cloud picnic.
PIP: “Thank you. We’ll plant kindness at home.”
GIANT: “That grows taller than gold.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
PIP: “Courage climbs; greed falls.”
NIB: “And cheese climbs into my pockets either way.”
GIANT: “Share the song. Keep the sky kind.”

### Page 9 — End
Back on the farm, a tiny beanstalk glow lights the garden.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: Courage climbs; greed falls.
