# Digi World Time & Rate Desk — START HERE

Track billable hours and know your real hourly rate — no timesheet SaaS required.
Time entries, rate calculator, and weekly totals — all on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Time-Rate-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — default hourly rate and currency
2. **Rate calculator** — desired income, hours/week, overhead % → suggested rate
3. **Dashboard** — hours this week, billable $, effective rate
4. **Log time** — date, client/project, hours, billable yes/no
5. Review the dashboard every Friday
6. Adjust your rate in Setup when the calculator says so
