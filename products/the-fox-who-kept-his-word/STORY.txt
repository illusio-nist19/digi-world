# THE FOX WHO KEPT HIS WORD
## Digi World · DW-EBK-013 · Ages 3–8
Wisdom: A promise is a bridge — keep it, and people trust you with bigger journeys.

Characters: Fenn (young fox), Dot (hedgehog), River Otter, Tempting Pie-Squirrel

### Cover
Title: THE FOX WHO KEPT HIS WORD
Subtitle: A woodland tale about promises & trust
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Sunny meadow; Fenn raises a paw.
FENN: “I promise I’ll bring the blueberries by sunset.”
DOT: “I believe you. That’s a big gift.”

### Page 2
Fenn trots into the woods with a basket.
Text: Promises feel light at noon… and heavy when snacks appear.

### Page 3 — Funny
Pie-Squirrel waves a giant berry pie.
SQUIRREL: “Trade? Your promise for one bite of destiny!”
FENN: “Destiny smells illegal.”

### Page 4
River rises; bridge wiggles.
OTTER: “Shortcut across if you drop the basket!”
FENN: “Then Dot waits hungry. No shortcuts on promises.”

### Page 5
Funny: basket tip; berries roll; Fenn does slapstick catch juggling.
FENN: “I’m a circus now. Professional.”

### Page 6
Storm clouds; Fenn wants to nap in a cozy den.
Tempting voice in his head: “Tomorrow is also a sunset…”
FENN: “Tomorrow is a different word.”

### Page 7
He helps Otter free a stuck oar — still keeps moving toward Dot.
OTTER: “You could’ve said ‘later.’”
FENN: “Later breaks bridges.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
Sunset paints the meadow pink; Fenn arrives with full basket.
DOT: “You came.”
FENN: “I said I would.”
Text: Kept words make small foxes feel tall.

### Page 9
Pie-Squirrel arrives ashamed with a tiny apology tart.
SQUIRREL: “I sell temptation. You’re bad for business… and good for friends.”

### Page 10
Village animals ask Fenn to carry messages — because he finishes what he starts.
OWL-ELDER: “Trust is earned in sunsets kept.”

### Page 11 — Funny dialogue
DOT: “Next promise: no juggling berries.”
FENN: “I promise… to try.”
DOT: “Hmm.”
FENN: “Fine. I promise. Fully.”

### Page 12
Night; Fenn and Dot share berries under lantern bugs.
Text: Easy promises are noise. Kept promises are music.

### Page 13
Fenn teaches kits: say less, mean more, finish always.

### Page 14 — Closing
Text: Your word is a path others walk on.
Build it strong — children who keep promises grow into leaders people follow.

### Page 15 — End
THE END
Wisdom: Mean what you say. Finish what you promise.
