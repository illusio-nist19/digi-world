# THE BRAVE LITTLE LANTERN
## Digi World · DW-EBK-011 · Ages 3–8
Wisdom: Courage is not no-fear — it’s taking the next kind step while afraid.

Characters: Lumi (small lantern spirit), Theo (boy), Squeak (nervous mouse), Fog-Beast (misunderstood)

### Cover
Title: THE BRAVE LITTLE LANTERN
Subtitle: A night adventure about real courage
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Twilight forest path; Theo holds Lumi, who flickers shyly.
THEO: “What if the dark is too big?”
LUMI: “Then we make a small bright.”

### Page 2
Squeak the mouse peeks from a mitten.
SQUEAK: “I vote we go home and invent daylight.”
LUMI: “Courage doesn’t need votes. It needs one step.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Theo trips on a root; Lumi bonks his nose with soft light.
LUMI: “Hero landing!”
THEO: “My dignity disagrees.”

### Page 4
Fog rolls in; shapes look scary.
Text: Fear draws dragons on fog. Kindness erases them.

### Page 5
They hear a roar — Fog-Beast lost and sniffling.
FOG-BEAST: “I can’t find my cave… everything looks like soup.”
SQUEAK: “Soup is my love language. Still scary.”

### Page 6
Funny dialogue:
THEO: “Are you… crying fog?”
FOG-BEAST: “It’s dramatic humidity!”

### Page 7
Lumi’s light shakes but she floats closer.
LUMI: “I’m scared too. We can be scared together — and helpful.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
They walk the beast home step by step.
Text: Bravery is a lantern: it doesn’t remove night. It shows the next root.

### Page 9
Fog-Beast thanks them with a flower that glows.
FOG-BEAST: “You didn’t fight me. You found me.”

### Page 10
Home path clearer; Theo’s shoulders drop.
THEO: “I thought brave meant loud.”
LUMI: “Brave can whisper.”

### Page 11 — Funny
Squeak wears a leaf as a cape.
SQUEAK: “Official sidekick. Salary: crumbs.”
LUMI: “Paid in courage. Expensive.”

### Page 12
Village kids listen; Theo shares the story without boasting.
Text: Quiet heroes still change the map.

### Page 13
Night window; Lumi glows on the sill.
THEO: “Next time I’m afraid…”
LUMI: “We take the kind next step.”

### Page 14 — Closing
Text: Courage isn’t a roar. It’s a small light that keeps walking.
Be brave enough to help — even when your knees wobble.

### Page 15 — End
THE END
Wisdom: Feel the fear. Do the next kind thing anyway.
