# Digi World Proposal & Pitch Desk — START HERE

Win more work without a CRM subscription. Proposals, clients, and pipeline value — all on this computer.
No cloud. No login. Data stays local.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Proposal-Pitch-Desk.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name and currency
2. **Dashboard** — open proposals, win rate, pipeline value
3. **Clients** — add the studios and brands you pitch to
4. **New proposal** — title, client, scope, line items → Save
5. Mark status **sent** when it goes out, **accepted** or **lost** when you know
6. **Print / PDF** — open the proposal → Print → Save as PDF
