# Digi World Testimonial Vault — START HERE

Collect, permission-track, and organize client quotes for your site and proposals.
Stars, source, and approval status — all offline on this computer.

## Open
1. Unzip this folder
2. Double-click **Testimonial-Vault.html**
3. Work in Chrome, Edge, or Safari

## First 10 minutes
1. **Setup** — business name
2. **Testimonials** — browse sample quotes and filters
3. **Editor** — add a new quote with stars and source
4. Set permission to pending until the client approves
5. **Dashboard** — approved count, average stars, pending permissions
6. Filter by status before copying quotes to your website
