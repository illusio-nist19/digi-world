# THE FRIENDSHIP POT
## Digi World · DW-EBK-016 · Ages 3–8
Inspired by the Stone Soup folktale.
Wisdom: When everyone adds a little, hungry becomes feast.

Characters: Chef Lila, Pot (cheerful), Hungry Forest Friends, Empty Bowl Blues

### Cover
Title: THE FRIENDSHIP POT
Subtitle: A stone-soup story about sharing
Tag: Digi World · Storybook
Scene: Big iron pot bubbling golden soup; girl chef stirring; fox, bunny, bear bringing veggies; steam hearts; colorful title.

### Page 1
Empty village square; tummies rumble like drums.
LILA: “No soup. No crumbs. Just one shiny stone.”
POT: “I’m empty… emotionally and soup-wise.”

### Page 2
Lila drops the stone in water and lights a fire.
LILA: “I’m cooking Friendship Soup!”
FOX: “Soup from a rock? Bold. Suspicious. I’m in.”

### Page 3 — Funny
Bunny peeks.
BUNNY: “Does it taste like pebble sadness?”
LILA: “Not yet. It needs a carrot of courage.”
Bunny tosses a carrot. Splash!

### Page 4
Bear brings barley; squirrel brings herbs.
BEAR: “I add strength.”
SQUIRREL: “I add tiny drama leaves!”
Steam rises happier.

### Page 5 — Dialogue
More friends arrive with onions, beans, bread.
LILA: “See? The pot wasn’t magic. You were.”
POT: “Rude. I am also magic. And shiny.”

### Page 6
They taste; eyes widen; bowls appear.
FOX: “This tastes like… everybody.”
BUNNY: “And not like rock. Thankfully.”

### Page 7
They set a long outdoor table under lanterns.
Empty Bowl Blues fades into a smile.
KID: “I only had one pea… and it mattered.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
LILA: “A closed pantry stays lonely. An open spoon feeds a street.”
POT: “Share a little. Grow a lot.”

### Page 9 — End
Night feast; leftovers packed for tomorrow’s travelers.
Text: THE END · Digi World
Wisdom: When everyone adds a little, hungry becomes feast.
