# The Secret Sleep Keeps
## An original Digi World bedtime story
Ages 3–7 · Wisdom: Rest is not wasted time — brave tomorrows grow while you sleep.

Characters: Milo (boy), Pip (sister), Spark (toy dragon), Scout (dog), Luma (shy night-moth)

---

### Cover
Title: THE SECRET SLEEP KEEPS
Subtitle: A cozy bedtime adventure about rest, wonder & tomorrow’s courage
Tag: Digi World · Storybook

### Page 1
Milo under teal covers, lamp warm, stars outside.
Text:
“Sleeping is boring!” Milo sighs at the sky.
“Why close my eyes when the night’s flying by?”
Pip hangs upside down with a mischievous grin.
“Then stay awake, Milo… and let the night in.”

### Page 2
Scout’s tail twirls at the window; curtains dance.
Text:
A soft wind whirls. The curtains all lean.
Scout’s little tail starts a happy machine!
Spark the toy dragon pops open one eye—
“If night wants a visit… we might as well try!”

### Page 3 — Dialogue funny
Spark sneezes glitter.
PIP: “Spark! You sneezed on my braid!”
SPARK: “Sorry! Moon pepper. It tickles my brain!”
MILO: “Is night… knocking?”
SCOUT: “Woof! (That means yes.)”

### Page 4
A glowing keyhole appears in the air by the lamp.
Text:
A shimmering keyhole floats near the light—
not scary, just soft, like a hush in the night.
Milo whispers, “What if we peek… just a bit?”
Pip grins, “One peek. Then we go where it fits.”

### Page 5
They step into a moon-garden of silver leaves.
Text:
They tip-toe through silver, through soft leafy lanes,
where fireflies practice their tiny refrains.
Funny: Spark trips on a mushroom and bows.
“Ta-da!” says the dragon. “I meant to do that… somehow.”

### Page 6
They meet Luma, a shy glowing moth with dim light.
LUMA: “I lost my glow… rushing too fast through the night.”
MILO: “We can help! We’ll chase every spark!”
PIP: “We’ll run and we’ll grab—”
LUMA: “Running made my light dark.”

### Page 7 — Funny fail
They zoom, grab, tumble; glitter chaos; still dim.
Text:
They raced. They grabbed. They giggled and fell.
They caught zero glow-beams (but caught Spark as well!).
Luma soft-laughs, “Brave hearts… but listen, my friends:
some magic gets louder when hurrying ends.”

### Page 8 — Wisdom
They sit, breathe, rest beside Luma.
Text:
So they sit. They breathe. They wait with the moth.
No chasing. No rushing. Just kindness and cloth—
a blanket of quiet the night likes to keep.
And slowly… Luma’s soft light starts to leap.

### Page 9
Luma glows; garden brightens; Scout howls happily.
LUMA: “Thank you. Rest helped me remember my shine.”
MILO: “Rest… helped?”
LUMA: “Yes. Brave tomorrows need nighttime to climb.”

### Page 10 — Wise + warm
Text:
“Sleep isn’t boring,” the garden winds sing.
“It’s how little hearts practice tomorrow’s brave wing.
You grow while you dream. You heal while you rest.
The quietest minutes can build you your best.”

### Page 11 — Funny
Spark tries to sleep mid-air and snores glitter bubbles.
PIP: “He’s sleeping… while floating?”
MILO: “That’s advanced, I think.”
SPARK (asleep): “Zzz… don’t… rush… the… snack…”

### Page 12
They say goodbye; keyhole softens back to bedroom.
Text:
They wave to the garden. The keyhole grows small.
Back under the covers, still cozy and tall.
Scout curls by the window. The lamp hums low.
Pip whispers, “Okay… I’m ready to go.”

### Page 13
Milo yawns, hugs Spark.
MILO: “If sleep keeps a secret… I want in the club.”
PIP: “Welcome. First rule: no rushing the hug.”
They giggle. Then quieter. Then quieter still.

### Page 14
Soft closing rhyme.
Text:
So if bedtime feels boring, remember this tune:
the night isn’t empty — it’s packing your moon.
Rest isn’t wasting the minutes you keep…
It’s how your tomorrow grows brave while you sleep.

### Page 15 — End / dedication
THE END
Wisdom for growing hearts:
Pause. Breathe. Rest. Then try again — kindly.
