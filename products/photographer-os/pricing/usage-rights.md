# Usage rights (commercial and brand)

The shoot fee pays for **making** the pictures. Usage pays for **where they work**.

Wedding and family portraits: personal use + sharing on the couple’s socials is enough. Brands running ads is a different product.

## Default (unless the proposal says otherwise)

- Studio keeps copyright.
- Client gets a **licence**: named channels, named term, named territory.
- Sub-licensing to media buyers / partners needs a line in the licence.

This is **not** a legal instrument. Your counsel turns this table into a contract.

## Price the use, not the JPEG count

| Use | Starting multiplier on shoot fee | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organic social / website, 12 months, one brand | +25–40% | Most “we just need photos for Instagram” jobs |
| Paid social / paid search, 6–12 months | +50–100% | The picture is now media inventory |
| Paid, 24 months | +100–150% | |
| Out-of-home / print campaign | Quote as estate | Circulation matters |
| Perpetual, all media | +200% or decline | Often a bad trade |
| Category exclusivity (e.g. no other coffee brand, 12 mo) | +50% on top of usage | You cannot shoot their rival |
| Buyout / assignment of copyright | 3–5× shoot or decline | You lose the archive |

**Worked example (fill your numbers):**

Full day shoot $2,400. Organic 12-month website+IG: +$800. Paid social 12-month: +$1,800. Total $4,200 before extras.

If an agency says “usage included, it’s just a small brand”: that is the tell. Small brands still run ads.

## Delivery gate

Commercial files go out when:

1. Shoot fee invoice is paid (or PO + agreed net)
2. Usage invoice is paid **or** usage is on the same invoice
3. Model/property releases are in your folder (client’s job unless you were hired to get them)

## Internal log

Put usage term and end date in `boards/jobs.csv` → `Usage_end`. One week before expiry, email: extend at [PRICE] or take the assets down from ads. Most studios never send this email. That is free money plus legal risk.
