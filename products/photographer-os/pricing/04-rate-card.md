# Rate card — how to publish prices without going broke

## Public vs private

- **Public** (website, Etsy cousin, IG story): starting prices and what is included.
- **Private** (`rate-card.csv`): floors, extra-hour, travel, second shooter, rush, raw licence.

Never publish the floor.

## Package architecture (three rungs)

| Rung | Job it does | Typical contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Door | Gets the yes from a real budget | Shorter hours, one location, no second, standard turnaround |
| House | Where profit lives | Full hours, second shooter *or* album credit, priority date |
| Estate | High-touch | Coverage + album + parent album / extra shooter / rehearsal |

Name rungs in *your* language. Do not call them Bronze/Silver/Gold.

## Wedding inclusions (write these in every proposal)

**Included:** coverage window, edited gallery of [MIN]–[MAX] images (you set a range, not “unlimited”), online gallery [MONTHS], print-release for personal use, [1] sneak-peek set within [DAYS].

**Not included:** extra hours, second shooter, engagement, albums, raws, drones, travel beyond [KM], meals, parking, reprints, rush.

## Extra-hour math

Extra hour = `(package price ÷ included hours) × 1.15`  
The 1.15 is fatigue and overtime edit. Round to a clean number.

## Travel

- Inside radius [KM]: included.
- Beyond: mileage + time at true hourly × 50% (you are not shooting).
- Overnight: hotel + per diem. Quote before you hold the date.

## Peak

Saturday peak months: add **[10–20%]** or close the door package. Do not “be nice” in September.

## How to fill `rate-card.csv`

One row per sellable thing. `Kind` = package | add-on | usage | fee.

After you fill it, open `tools/package-calculator.html` and quote a fake job. If the contribution looks like a bad assisting day, raise the package, not the coffee budget.
