# Chase scripts

Keep the tone of a studio, not a collections agency. Then stop working.

**+3 days**  
Hi [NAME] — invoice [ID] for [JOB] is still open ([AMOUNT]). Pay link: [URL]. Gallery / date holds when this clears.

**+7 days**  
Following up: the balance was due [DATE]. I pause edits (or I do not send the gallery password) until it is paid. Link: [URL].

**+14 days**  
Final note on [ID]. If I do not have payment by [DATE], I release the remaining work and the date is no longer reserved for reprints / extra hours. [URL].

Commercial AP: CC the producer and the AP email from the questionnaire. Brands pay when the right inbox is on the thread.
