# Wedding shot list (studio, not Pinterest)

This is a **coverage checklist**, not 400 poses. Tick on the printed sheet.

## Must (if it exists on the day)

- Rings, shoes, dress on hanger, vows/letters, invitation
- Each person getting ready (faces, not only hands)
- Full dress / full suit
- First look or first touch
- Walk down, vows, kiss, recessional
- Family formals (from the signed list)
- Couple portraits (wide, tight, walking)
- Room wide (empty or full)
- Tablescape / stationery only if the couple asked
- Speeches (speaker + listeners)
- First dance, parent dances
- Cake / ritual they named
- Exit

## Should

- Vendor heroes (one frame each they will actually post)
- Guests who were named in the questionnaire
- Night detail of the room

## Never chase

- Every table’s group shot unless it is in the package as “table tours” (that is an add-on hour)
- The DJ’s lighting tricks at the expense of faces

Commercial / portrait lists are shorter: `shot-list-commercial.md`.
