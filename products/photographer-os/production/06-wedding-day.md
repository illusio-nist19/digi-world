# Wedding-day production

## Before you leave home

- Two camera bodies, overlapping lenses, extra batteries, extra cards, dual-slot if you have it
- Rain: clear umbrella, plastic, towel, backup indoor portraits location from questionnaire
- Printed timeline + formals list (phones die)
- Second shooter brief signed off

## On site

| Block | You |
| --- | --- |
| Getting ready | Details, dress, people. Do not eat 40 minutes of “flat lay art” if the couple wanted faces. |
| First look / ceremony | Two angles if you have a second. Ceremony: no walking the aisle unless you were told you may. |
| Formals | `family-formals.md` — 15 min |
| Golden hour / couple | Protect this in the timeline. If cocktail runs long, you still pull them. That is what they hired. |
| Reception | Speeches, dance, room. Extra hour is prepaid or you leave. |

## Dual backup rule (non-negotiable)

Before you drive away: cards copied to laptop **and** a second drive (or ingest to a phone/SSD you do not keep in the same bag as the cards). A photographer who “lost the wedding” is not a branding story you survive.

## After

- Sleep. Cull starts [next day / two days] — write your rule so clients stop asking at 01:00.
