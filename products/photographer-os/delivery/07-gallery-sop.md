# Gallery SOP

## Turnaround (publish these numbers)

| Job | Sneak peek | Full gallery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Portrait | 48–72h | 10–14 days |
| Wedding | 5–7 days (10–20 images) | 5–7 weeks |
| Commercial | Next business day (hero 3) | Spec in the proposal |

Rush = add-on from the rate card. Do not “just this once.”

## Preview → revisions → full

1. Upload a **preview set** (40–80 images for a wedding; 10–15 for portraits).
2. Client has **[7] days** and **two rounds** of notes. Notes must be specific (“lose images 12, 18; brighter on 4”). “Make it more magical” is not a round — ask them to pick frames.
3. You apply notes. Round 3+ is [PRICE] per round.
4. Full gallery goes live. Personal print-release in the gallery footer.

If they hate the set: that is a **reshoot conversation** with a fee, or a referral to another editor — not unlimited recull.

## Hosting

- Live [12] months (wedding house). Then archive download available [30] days, after that retrieval fee [PRICE].
- You also keep studio archive [years] per your insurance / disk SOP.

## Paid-in-full gate

Gallery password is sent when `invoices` for that job show balance **Paid**. Not before. If they have a speech next week and have not paid: they pay, then they speak.

## Vendor sneak

One image per key vendor within sneak-peek window, watermarked or not — your call — with a request to credit `[STUDIO]`. This is how next year’s Saturday fills.
