# Raw files

**Default: not included.** Edited JPEGs (and prints) are the product.

If you ever sell raws:

- Price: **[2–3×]** the package or a flat [PRICE] that hurts
- Licence: personal archive only, no commercial, no third-party edit sold as yours
- Delivery after paid-in-full and after the edited gallery is accepted
- You still keep copyright unless counsel writes an assignment

Most clients asking for raws are afraid. The preview + cap + print-release usually ends it. The ones who insist after the price are not your clients.
