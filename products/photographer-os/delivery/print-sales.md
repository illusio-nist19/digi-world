# Print and album sales

Open the print store **when the full gallery goes live**, not six months later.

- 3–5 curated albums (you pick, they approve) convert better than 900 images all set to 4×6.
- Parent album: sell at booking as an add-on. After the day, emotion is high but budget is tired.
- In-person / video reveal: 20 minutes. If you hate selling, a print credit already in the House package does the work.

Log print revenue in `money/invoice-log.csv` as kind `print` so you see whether albums are a product or a hobby.
