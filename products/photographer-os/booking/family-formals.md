# Family formals — 15 minutes, 20 frames

Collect **T-14**. If the list is 40 combinations, you will blow the cocktail hour. Cut with them on the timeline call.

Default set (edit for culture / religion / blended families):

1. Couple
2. Couple + couple’s children
3. Couple + her parents
4. Couple + his parents
5. Couple + both sets of parents
6. Bride + her parents
7. Groom + his parents
8. Bride + siblings
9. Groom + siblings
10. Full her family
11. Full his family
12. Grandparents with couple (each side)
13. Wedding party
14. Wedding party + couple
15. Officiant with couple (optional)
16. Anyone travelling who will never be in one room again (max 3 extras)

**Rules**

- One marshall (planner or sibling) with the list. You do not shout names.
- 15 minutes on the clock. After that you are in cocktail coverage.
- No “one more with cousins from the other hotel.” That is a portrait session you will sell later.

Hand this list to the second shooter: they run a second camera on the same groups, not a creative side quest.
