# Wedding questionnaire

Send as a Google Form / Typeform. Every answer maps to a job note.

## People

- Couple names as they should appear on the gallery
- Phone (each)
- Planner / coordinator name + phone
- Getting-ready addresses (A and B)
- Ceremony venue + indoor backup
- Reception venue
- Who is paying the studio invoice?

## Day

- First look? (Y/N) If no, family formals after ceremony — confirm with planner.
- Officiant flash/no-flash rules
- Unplugged ceremony? (Y/N)
- Must-have family combinations: attach `family-formals.md`
- People who should not be photographed together
- Children / elders who need a shorter formals block
- Exit (sparklers, petals, nothing) — venue rules
- Private vows / letters?

## Vendors you must greet

Florist, HMUA, band/DJ, caterer, cake — names. You will make one image that each vendor actually shares. That is unpaid marketing you control.

## Logistics

- Parking / load-in
- Meal for photographer(s) — if no meal, you leave to eat and coverage dips. Write it.
- End time the venue forces (you leave then, extra hour cannot fight a lock)

## Look

- 8 reference images from **your** site (not Pinterest strangers)
- Colours / film vs clean
- Anything you refuse (heavy flash on the aisle, drone over guests, etc.)
