# Booking SOP

A job is **Lead** until retainer is in. Then it is **Booked**. Never the other way around.

## Sequence

1. Qualifier complete (`inquiry/qualifier.md`).
2. Proposal sent: package + add-ons + travel + (commercial) usage. Valid **[14] days**.
3. They say yes. You send: agreement (counsel-reviewed) + retainer invoice + questionnaire.
4. **Retainer clears** → date marked Booked on the board → welcome packet the same day.
5. If retainer does not clear in **[72] hours**, the date is released. Tell them in writing.

## Questionnaire timing

- Wedding: send at booking. **Chase at T-30 days** if incomplete. Formals list due **T-14 days**.
- Portrait: send with booking. Due **48h before**.
- Commercial: brief + shot owner named **5 days before** or you bill pre-pro.

Files: `questionnaire-wedding.md`, `questionnaire-commercial.md`, `family-formals.md`.

## What “Booked” requires in the board

- Client row
- Job row with date, package, hours, second shooter Y/N
- Invoice row: retainer, status Paid
- Next action date (questionnaire / timeline call)

## Timeline call (weddings, 20 minutes)

When: T-21 to T-14.  
Agenda: ceremony rules, formals block (15 min), golden hour, vendor meal, sparkler/exit, who has the rings, who is late by nature.

Write the run-of-show in `production/timeline.md`. Send a one-page PDF to couple **and** planner.

## Cancellation (operating default — counsel must localise)

- Retainer: held as liquidated inventory cost (wording is legal).
- If *you* cancel: full refund + you help find cover. Do not become the story.
- Date change: one courtesy move if the new date is open, inside [12] months. Second move = new retainer.

## Second shooter

Book them when the job is Booked, not the week of. Brief: `production/second-shooter.md`. Pay on your SOP (deposit + shoot day).
