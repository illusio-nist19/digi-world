This is not a Notion wallpaper. It is the operating system of a photography studio that books, shoots, delivers, and collects — without leaking hours into “unlimited revisions,” unpaid scoping, and galleries that never close.

Generic “freelancer CRM” templates fail photographers because a wedding is not a logo project. A brand campaign is not a family session. Dates are inventory. Saturdays are finite. Usage rights are where commercial profit lives. This kit is built against those jobs.

## What “done” looks like

After setup you can answer yes to all of these:

1. Every inquiry gets a **15-minute reply** (or a scheduled auto-reply that still sounds human).
2. You have **three named packages** per line (wedding / portrait / commercial) with hours, deliverables, and what is *not* included.
3. **Usage rights** for commercial work are priced separately from the shoot day.
4. A job cannot be marked Booked until **retainer is logged**.
5. Preview gallery has a **revision cap** (two rounds). Extra rounds have a fee.
6. **No files leave the studio** until the balance is paid.
7. You know, this Saturday, whether you are **at capacity**.
8. You can send a **welcome packet** the same day the retainer clears.

If you cannot show those eight artefacts, you have a pretty dashboard. You do not have a studio.

## Who this is for

Working photographers who:

- book **weddings, portraits, or commercial/brand** work (one line or a hybrid studio)
- lose jobs to slow DMs, vague “it depends” quotes, or unpaid extra hours
- already shoot — they need the **desk**, not a lighting course
- will install this in Notion **or** Google Sheets (both are first-class)

Solo, duo (you + second shooter), or a 2–4 person studio.

## Who this is not for

- Someone who has never photographed a paying client (get three paid jobs, then install this)
- A videographer-only shop (the shot lists and gallery rules will fight you)
- Anyone who wants a lawyer-drafted contract for their jurisdiction (we give **operating checklists**; counsel reviews the actual agreement)
- Resellers (the licence is **one studio**)

## The only rule that matters on day one

**No date is held, and no file is delivered, until money that matches the SOP is in.**

Everything else in this kit exists to make that rule polite, written, and repeatable.
