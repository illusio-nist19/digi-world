# The 15-minute reply

Speed is the product until they have seen your work. After they have seen your work, **clarity** is the product.

## SLA

| When inquiry arrives | You do |
| --- | --- |
| Studio hours (define: e.g. 09:00–18:00 local) | Reply in **15 minutes** or less |
| Evenings / Sunday | Auto-reply: you will answer by 10:00 next studio morning — then actually do it |
| Peak season (name your months) | Same SLA. If you cannot, you are over capacity. Stop ads. |

Saved replies live in Instagram, Gmail templates, and your website form autoresponder. Same skeleton. Change only the names.

## Skeleton (copy, then fill)

**Subject / first line:** `[DATE] — [STUDIO] here`

Thank you for writing — I have [DATE] on the board.

To send the right two packages, I only need:

1. Location / venue  
2. Hours you want coverage (ceremony → last dance, or “portraits only”)  
3. Guest count (approx.)  
4. Is this a wedding, elopement, portrait, or brand/commercial?

Two starting places for a [CITY] [TYPE] on that date:

- **[PACKAGE A NAME]** — [HOURS]h, [DELIVERABLES one line], [PRICE]  
- **[PACKAGE B NAME]** — [HOURS]h, [DELIVERABLES], [PRICE]

If one of those is in range, I will send the lookbook and a 15-minute call link. I do not hold dates without a retainer.

— [NAME], [STUDIO]

## Rules

- **Two packages**, not seven. Paradox of choice kills wedding inquiries.
- Do not attach a 12-page PDF in the first reply. Link to 8–12 images or a 60-second film.
- If they ask “how much for a wedding?” with no date: send the **starting** 8-hour number and the qualifier questions. Do not write “it depends” as the first sentence.
- If the date is gone: say so in sentence one. Offer a weekday, a second shooter associate (if you have one), or a portrait session. Do not ghost.

## Qualify-out (polite)

If budget is named and it is under your floor:

I would not be the right photographer at that number — I would rather say so now than compress the day. Here are two studios I trust in [CITY]: [NAME], [NAME].

Referrals make you look expensive in the correct way.

## 2-hour and 24-hour follow-up

If they engaged but did not book a call:

**T+2h:** One line: “Happy to hold questions if you want the lookbook first — here it is: [LINK].”

**T+24h:** “I still have [DATE] unmarked. I will release it [DAY] evening if I have not had a retainer.” Then release it. Scarcity that you do not honour trains people to wait.
