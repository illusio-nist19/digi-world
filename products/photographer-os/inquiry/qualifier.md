# Inquiry qualifier

Use on the 15-minute call or as a form **after** the first reply. Ten minutes. You are listening for constraints, not performing.

## Score (circle)

| Signal | Hot | Warm | Walk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Date | Open on your board | Same weekend, you have a maybe | You are already booked / travelling |
| Budget vs floor | At or above starting package | 10–20% below — they can stretch to a shorter day | Named number under floor |
| Decision | Couple (or brand + marketing) on the call | One person “I’ll ask them” | Committee of twelve, no owner |
| Location | Your city / usual radius | 1–3h travel (quote travel) | Fly + visa + unknown permit |
| Style match | They mention your actual galleries | “We just need someone with a camera” | They want a look you do not make |
| Timeline | Booking 4+ months out (wedding) or 3+ weeks (commercial) | Tight but possible | “This Saturday” with no brief |

**Hot:** send proposal same day.  
**Warm:** send one smaller package + one full.  
**Walk:** refer out. Protect Saturday inventory.

## Questions (ask in this order)

1. What should I not miss — the thing you would be angry about if it was not photographed?
2. Who else is covering (friend with a camera, uncle, content creator)?
3. Flash during ceremony — venue rules?
4. Ready-by time for portraits / family formals?
5. Who pays (couple / parents / brand / agency)?
6. When do you need the gallery live (must-have vs nice-to-have)?

Write answers into `boards/jobs.csv` field `Notes`.

## Commercial extras

- Paid ads or organic only? (this is a **usage** question — see `pricing/usage-rights.md`)
- Exclusivity in the category?
- Model releases already handled?
- Shot list owner on their side (name + WhatsApp/email)?
