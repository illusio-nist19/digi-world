# Objection map

Do not argue. Translate. Then offer one next step.

| They say | What it usually is | You say | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| “You’re expensive.” | No comparison set | “Against a second shooter + 40 hours of edit, the number is the job. Here is what is *not* in a cheaper day.” | Send 8-hour vs 6-hour side by side |
| “We have a friend with a camera.” | Risk they do not want to name | “Friends are for candids. Formals, light, and a gallery that does not die on a phone — that is the studio.” | Keep friend as guest; you still own formals block |
| “Can you just do 2 hours?” | Budget | “Two hours is a portrait or an elopement coverage, not a wedding. Here is that package.” | Requote the real job |
| “We need unlimited photos / all the raws.” | Fear of missing | “You get every *deliverable* in the package, edited. Raws are a separate licence if I offer them at all.” | Point to raw policy |
| “Can you throw in an engagement shoot free?” | Testing boundaries | “Engagement is Package Add-on [PRICE]. I do not discount the wedding to win the add-on.” | Add-on or no |
| “We’ll decide after we see another three studios.” | Not ready | “Take the week. I release [DATE] on [DAY] if there is no retainer.” | Calendar reminder to release |
| “Can you edit like [Instagram stranger]?” | Style mismatch | “I will not. Here is my gallery. If that is not the film you want, I will refer you.” | Refer |
| “Invoice net 60.” (brand) | Their AP | “Net 14, 50% to hold the crew. Files on payment.” | PO + dates in writing |
| “Can we get extra hours on the night if we feel it?” | Scope creep incoming | “Extra hour is [PRICE], booked 7 days out, not decided at 23:00.” | Write it in the proposal |
| “Our last photographer lost the files.” | Trust | “Dual backup before I leave the venue. Gallery hosted [MONTHS]. I do not deliver a USB as the only copy.” | Backup SOP one-liner |

If you hear three Walk signals from `qualifier.md`, stop selling.
