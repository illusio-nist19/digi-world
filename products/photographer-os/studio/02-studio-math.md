# Studio math — capacity and true hourly

Fill this once. Revisit every January and after any price change.

A studio that “feels busy” and still cannot pay rent is usually selling hours at a fake rate: they quoted the shoot day and donated the cull, the emails, the travel, and the gallery.

## 1. Saturday inventory (weddings)

Peak wedding studios are constrained by **Saturdays**, not by cameras.

If bookable Saturdays = 18, you do not have a marketing problem when 18 are held. You have a **yield** problem: are those 18 the right packages?

Portrait and commercial fill **weekdays**. Do not price weekday work as if it were Saturday scarcity.

## 3. True hourly

```
True hourly = (Package price − second shooter − extra hours sold − travel costs − gallery/hosting − ads allocated − payment fees)
              ÷ total hours from section 2
```

## 5. The 40% rule

Once a date is held, **≥40%** of the package should be non-refundable retainer (or the maximum your counsel allows in your country). Remaining balance due **14–30 days before** the wedding, or **on the shoot day** for portraits, or **net 7–14** for commercial with PO.

If a couple “needs to think” after you have taken the date off the market with no retainer, you donated inventory.
