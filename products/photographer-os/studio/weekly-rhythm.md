# Weekly studio rhythm (Monday, 45 minutes)

Same 45 minutes every week. Phone in another room.

1. **Pipeline** (10) — inquiries older than 48h: reply or release. Dates without retainer: release.
2. **This week’s jobs** (10) — questionnaires complete? Second shooter confirmed? Cards / batteries?
3. **Money** (10) — overdue invoices: send the matching chase script. Usage_end in the next 14 days: send extend-or-takedown.
4. **Delivery** (10) — sneak peeks and galleries that slipped the SLA: do them or message a new honest date.
5. **Capacity** (5) — next 8 Saturdays: open / held / booked. If 6+ booked in peak, pause ads.

This meeting is the product. The databases only serve it.
