## The job this kit does

When an inquiry lands at 22:14 on a Thursday, you do not invent a studio. You run a desk: qualify, quote two packages, hold the date with a retainer, shoot against a list, deliver against a cap, collect the balance, then close the gallery.

## What this kit does not do

- Teach you lighting, posing, or Lightroom
- Replace a solicitor / attorney in your country
- Guarantee bookings
- Work as a multi-studio franchise pack (buy a licence per studio)
