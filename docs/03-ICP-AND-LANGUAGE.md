# 03 — ICP and language

## Primary ICPs (paid social)

We run UGC + AI video + edited ads on **Snapchat and TikTok** (Meta too). One store, four buyer minds. Copy rotates by collection, not by building four sites.

### ICP A — The Creator who is tired

- 18–34, any country, phone-first
- Posts or wants to post. Inconsistent. Consumes “how to go viral” at 1am.
- Pain: looking unserious, wasting hours, zero system
- Desire: a studio in their pocket, hooks that work, faceless option
- Buys: Creator OS, Faceless Studio, Hook Vault
- Emotion: shame of being “almost” + hunger to be seen

### ICP B — The Operator / side-business brain

- 22–45, solopreneur, freelancer, small DTC, student with a hustle
- Pain: messy ChatGPT chats, no offer, no money picture
- Desire: an AI that behaves like staff, a real offer, a money OS
- Buys: AI Operator, Offer Engine, Wealth OS
- Emotion: overwhelm + need for control / status of “I run this properly”

### ICP C — Glow / private life (women-heavy on Snap)

- 18–35, beauty/self-image/ritual
- Pain: starting over every Sunday, consuming content not changing
- Desire: a private ritual that makes her feel expensive
- Buys: Glow Ritual, Time Command as add-on
- Emotion: self-worth, being chosen by herself, quiet luxury

### ICP D — Gifters / duo buyers

- Buys 2 licenses at $29 (“لك ولصاحبتك / for you + someone you want to win”)
- High AOV path. Always show Duo on PDP.

## Saudi dialect rules (marketing)

Write like a sharp Riyadh/Jeddah ad, not Gulf cartoon and not stiff MSA.

**Do:**
- مو، الحين، صدق، خلّك، يبيلك، على طول، تمام، والله، يا بعدي (sparingly)
- Short lines. Periods. One idea per line.
- Gender: default **you** as masculine for mixed creator/operator pages; Glow Ritual uses feminine تاء (تبين، نفسكِ, جربي).
- Numbers and prices stay `19$` or `$19` with USD clearly marked. Prefer `$19` with `USD` microcopy.

**Don’t:**
- Heavy Bedouin slang that UAE/Egypt can’t parse
- Egyptian meme dialect
- Religious over-claim (“هذا بركة رزقك”) — we are a store, stay respectful and secular-commercial
- Fake تاء الكسرة on operator products

## English rules

- No slang that dates (rizz, gyatt).
- No fake British posh.
- Use “you”. Use “license” not “download” on CTAs (download can live in thank-you).
- Prefer verbs: Run. Install. Open the vault.

## Proof language vs hype

Allowed specific claims (use on site):

- “Built as a sequenced system (not a file dump).”
- “Studio-certified before a drop goes live.”
- “Instant vault after you confirm your email.”
- Behavioral science (cite plainly, no fake DOI):
  - Implementation intentions (Gollwitzer) — systems beat goals
  - Identity-based habits (Clear / self-perception)
  - Social proof (Cialdini)
  - Scarcity must be **true** (license pool per drop)

Forbidden:

- “Guaranteed 10k followers”
- “Make $10k in 7 days”
- Fake universities, fake ISO unless you actually have it
- Fake reviewer names that are celebrities

## Inclusive global

Do not assume Islam, Gulf, or US. Do not exclude them either. Imagery and testimonials should include Arabic names **and** international names. Currency USD. Shipping: digital, worldwide, instant.
