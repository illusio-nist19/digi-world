## Science you may cite (honest, short)

Put a **Science strip** on Home and PDPs. No fake papers.

1. **Implementation intentions** — people who install a when/then system act more than people who only set goals (Gollwitzer). *That’s why we ship an OS, not a motivational PDF.*
2. **Identity** — behavior sticks when it matches “who I am” (self-perception). *That’s why the brand is a house you join, not a file you hide.*
3. **Cognitive load** — choice overload kills follow-through. *One vault. Sequenced. Start-here first.*
4. **Social proof** — uncertain buyers copy others (Cialdini). *Licenses issued, reviews, UGC.*
5. **Scarcity** — only if true. *License pool per drop.*
6. **Endowed progress** — show “Step 1 of 3” in delivery email.

Never invent “Harvard 2024 study shows our PDF increases income 40%”.
