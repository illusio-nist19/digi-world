# 02 — Positioning

## The market we refuse

Etsy / Gumroad 2026 is flooded with:

- Generic planners and Notion dashboards
- “500 ChatGPT prompts” PDFs
- Canva template packs
- Low-price race ($3–$9)

Those convert on search. They do **not** convert on Snapchat/TikTok at a premium, and they do not support authority pricing.

## The market we take

People who already wasted money on files that look like homework. They want something that feels **designed, sequenced, and official**. They will pay more if it looks like a product Apple would ship — and if the ad already sold the identity.

**Positioning statement**

> For ambitious people who want leverage (creators, operators, and women building a private glow-up), Digi World is the studio that issues original digital operating systems — not templates — so you stop collecting files and start running a system. Unlike marketplace sellers, we brand, certify, and support what we ship.

## Enemy

The $7 PDF that sits in Drive forever. The “mega bundle” of 400 mismatched Canva files. The prompt pack with no workflow. The feeling of being behind while buying more junk.

## Promise

You open one vault. You follow one system. You feel in control within the first session.

## Why we can charge more

| Cheap file | Digi World |
| --- | --- |
| Random cover | Studio photography, serial number |
| 1 PDF | Multi-file OS (guides + swipe + calendars + SOP) |
| No story | Origin, certification, proof, science |
| No ladder | Solo / duo / pair / vault |
| No aftercare | Delivery ritual + thank-you confirmation |
| Looks like everyone | Looks like a house brand |

Price is justified by **identity + completeness + certification**, not by page count.

## Category design

Do not call them “digital products” on the homepage hero. Call them:

- EN: **Digital systems**
- AR: **أنظمة رقمية**

Support line:

- EN: *Built once in our studio. Licensed to you. Instantly.*
- AR: *انصنع في استوديو Digi World. ترخيصك أنت. يوصلك فوري.*

## Competitive frame (ads + PDP)

Never compare to a named competitor. Compare to the **category**:

- “This is not a planner.”
- “This is not 1000 prompts dumped in a PDF.”
- “This is the operating system those files were trying to be.”

## Global, not local-only

We sell worldwide. Imagery must not be only thobe/abaya or only Western. Mix:

- Hands, desks, phones, gold light, night city, studio, skin, notebooks
- Arabic + English UI chrome in mockups
- No flag-waving, no “for Saudis only”

Saudi dialect is **how we speak** to a large paid-social ICP. It is not a geographic fence.

## Authority pillars (must appear on Home + PDP)

1. **Studio original** — we design the system
2. **Certified drop** — numbered release, quality bar
3. **Proof** — reviews, “licenses issued”, UGC quotes
4. **Science** — tiny, true behavioral claims (implementation intention, identity, scarcity as honesty). No fake journals.
5. **Delivery ritual** — vault, email, what happens in 2 minutes
