# 23 — Etsy, Redbubble, other platforms

The **website is the brand house**. Other platforms are **distribution**, not the identity.

## Rule

Never upload the same ZIP as a $7 Etsy file. That trains the market you’re cheap and kills paid social.

## Etsy (search demand)

List **cousins**, not the OS:

- One-page printables derived from a system (desk pad, 1 ritual card)
- Price $6–$12
- Title: “Digi World · Glow Ritual desk card (printable)”
- Link in shop announcement: “Full systems at digi-world.online”
- Branding on the printable: gold circle DW

High-demand Etsy categories we **touch lightly**: planners, trackers. We do **not** compete as a generic planner shop.

## Redbubble / POD

Merch of the **circle mark** and wordmark (if the POD ToS allows trademark-style). Posters “Digital systems. Not files.” This is ads creative in the physical world, not the product catalog.

## Gumroad / Stan

Avoid. Splits brand. If needed later, one “Vault” link that 301s to the site.

## Amazon KDP

Out of scope.

## Why this catalog (seller rationale)

| Niche | Demand 2026 | Generic competition | Our angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creator / UGC / faceless | Very high | High on Etsy PDFs | Branded OS + bilingual hooks |
| AI operating system | Very high | Extreme on “prompt packs” | Staff/roles/quality gates, not prompts dump |
| Offers / sales system | High | Medium | DTC-native, we actually use this |
| Wealth OS | Evergreen | Extreme planners | Operator money, not cute budget |
| Glow ritual | High on Snap | High wellness PDFs | Private luxury, feminine AR |
| Time/attention | High | Extreme Notion | Named system, photography, certification |

We picked **few SKUs** so ads can hammer 3 heroes: Creator OS, AI Operator, Glow Ritual — then AOV via duo / pair / cart / upsell / vault.
